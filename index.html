Plugins

The server has many plugins them and they're commands/features are listed here.

01

AxGraves

/death tp, or, /grave tp, also to see all graves do /grave list, or, /death list

02

LandClaims

You claim land with a golden shovel and can right click the edges after creation to move them. You can use a stick to see who owns a claim. /trust, and, /unclaim

03

SkinsRestorer

/skin menu and /skin favorite

04

PkBetterItemFrames

Crouch and sneak click a item frame with shears to make it invisible and break it and replace it to make it visible or crouch click with a brush to restore visibility. Also Crouch click with a glass pane to lock rotation (items can still be removed) break and replace to remove the lock.

TRAVEL!

AtriusXWaystones

Craft a waystone key and link it to your lodestone. Surround it with ore blocks ranging in different boost amounts. To use waypoints between dimensions use a respawn anchor below the lodestone. Respawn anchor must be powered.
